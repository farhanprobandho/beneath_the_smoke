# Project brief — Beneath the Smoke

## Who / why
Audience: general public; problem: interpreting the 2026 Indonesian fire season through satellite observations.
Core story: (1) documented 2026 fires in West Kalimantan, (2) thermal detections, (3) independently mapped burned footprint, (4) how NISAR radar sees the surface differently, (5) what we can and cannot infer.

## Scope for ONE DAY
- One region, selected only after verifying both fire and NISAR geographic overlap.
- P0: deployable static React site with map, labels, data source credits, event timeline, and at least one actual NISAR image if one can be verified.
- P0: real thermal detections and a verified georeferenced burned area if available.
- P1: compatible NISAR pre/post change layer and one real readout. Make this conditional on verified pairs.
- P2: optical swipe, elaborate animation, additional locations.

## Do NOT build
Nationwide search, live NISAR processing API, fire forecasts, real-time alerts, fake wildfire simulations, or claims of underground peat-fire detection.

## Preferred story
Title: Beneath the Smoke. Chapters: The fires; Where fires were detected; What satellites saw; Interpreting the evidence. Honest provenance and uncertainty remain visible.
